About Alex

One teacher, 15 years of adult learners online, and every lesson plan on this site written by the same person.

Hi there, I'm Alex! Back when I was a student, a teacher of mine used to scribble on the board: "Learn without learning!" It completely flipped my perspective on education. I followed in those footsteps and became an English tutor myself — until I thought, why not turn the tables? "Teach without teaching!" I've spent the last 15 years "not teaching" and having a blast instead.

I specialise in teaching adult learners online — professionals, executives, and curious minds aged 25-55 who want real conversations, not textbook drills. My students have ranged from Russian IT engineers to French marketing directors to Brazilian entrepreneurs. What they all have in common: they're smart, busy, and bored of Spotlight and English File. So I build my own ESL lesson plans for adults filled with original topics and a healthy spoonful of weirdness and quirks.

My goal is to help online ESL tutors break out of that rigid routine and inspire them to create their own worlds. Our profession offers unmatched freedom and the chance to build genuine bonds with our students — no, wait, I call them "friends!"

Everything here is tested on real students before it is published. Each plan takes up to 6 hours to build: the topic, the reading, the audio, the questions, the vocabulary, the answer key.

What I actually do

  • Teach adults online, one to one and in small groups — still taking students.
  • Write every lesson plan in the library myself. No committee, no freelancers, no AI-generated filler.
  • Build each plan around a real article, video or podcast, so the conversation has something concrete to stand on.
  • Keep 26 plans completely free, no account required.
