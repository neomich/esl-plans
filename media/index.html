Copyright, media credits and takedown

Some lessons reference third-party images, audio and video for teaching purposes. If you own the rights to something here and believe it is used without permission, email esl-plans@proton.me with the lesson name and the item, and we will remove or replace it within 48 hours.

Why third-party material is here

These lessons are built around real material — a news photograph, a film scene, a song, a podcast, a social post — because adults stop listening when a class runs on invented dialogues. Some of that material belongs to other people.

Images, audio and video that come from third parties remain the property of their rights holders. They are included as part of a teaching resource — not offered as standalone assets, not sublicensed to buyers, and not licensed for reuse. Where the source, author or licence is known, it is credited on the lesson page or inside the lesson file.

If you own the rights

Write to esl-plans@proton.me and include:

  • the lesson name or the page URL;
  • the item itself — a link to the original work, or a short description of the image, track or clip;
  • confirmation that you own the rights, or that you are acting for the owner.

We will remove or replace the item within 48 hours of a verified request, and confirm by email when it is done. If the item is inside a downloadable pack, the pack is updated too, so new buyers do not receive it.

A short email is enough. You do not need a lawyer, a formal notice or a specific template to start the conversation.

If you are not sure

Write anyway. We would rather remove something than argue about it, and a replacement costs us less than a dispute.
