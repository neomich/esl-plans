ESL Conversation Questions About Psychology

Twenty-four questions about fear, habits, personality and memory — the kind of psychology that adults argue about over coffee. No theory, no jargon, just things people have opinions about.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Fears and anxiety

  1. What is the strangest thing you have ever been afraid of?
  2. Do you think fear is ever useful? When has fear protected you?
  3. Why do some people enjoy horror films and others cannot watch them?
  4. What do you do when you cannot sleep because you are worrying?
  5. Is anxiety talked about more openly now than when you were a child?
  6. Would you rather be fearless or cautious for the rest of your life?

Habits and willpower

  1. What habit did you try to break, and how long did you last?
  2. Why is it easy to start something in January and impossible by March?
  3. Do you trust people who say they have no bad habits?
  4. What small habit has improved your life more than any big decision?
  5. Does willpower actually exist, or is it just a good night’s sleep?
  6. What would you do differently if you had unlimited discipline for one month?

Personality and self-image

  1. How different is the person you are at work from the person you are at home?
  2. Do personality tests tell you anything true, or are they just flattering?
  3. What do people usually get wrong about you when they first meet you?
  4. Can people genuinely change, or do they only get better at hiding?
  5. Which compliment do you find hardest to accept?
  6. If a friend described you in three words, what would you hope they were?

Memory and perception

  1. What is your earliest memory, and how much of it do you trust?
  2. Why do we remember embarrassing moments so much better than happy ones?
  3. Have you ever completely changed your mind about a person? What happened?
  4. Do you think two people can remember the same event correctly but differently?
  5. What do you deliberately choose not to remember?
  6. If you could delete one memory, would you?
