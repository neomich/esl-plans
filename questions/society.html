ESL Conversation Questions About Society

Class, manners, generations and belonging — questions that split a room in half within thirty seconds. Useful for B1-C1 groups who are bored of safe topics.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Status and class

  1. Can you tell someone’s social class from the way they speak?
  2. Is it rude to ask what someone does for a living?
  3. Does money buy respect in your country, or does something else?
  4. What is the most obvious sign that someone grew up wealthy?
  5. Would you rather be respected or liked?
  6. Is social mobility really possible, or is it a story we tell?

Rules and rudeness

  1. What behaviour is normal in your country but rude somewhere else?
  2. Where is the line between being direct and being rude?
  3. Do people follow rules because they agree with them or because they are watched?
  4. What rule in your country would you abolish tomorrow?
  5. Is it ever acceptable to lie to be polite?
  6. What is the rudest thing a stranger has done to you?

Generations

  1. Which generation had it hardest, and why?
  2. What does the older generation misunderstand about yours?
  3. What do younger people get wrong about being older?
  4. Do you feel your age, or does the number surprise you?
  5. What did you believe at twenty that you find ridiculous now?
  6. Which generational complaint is actually justified?

Belonging and outsiders

  1. What makes someone an outsider in a group?
  2. Have you ever been the only foreigner in a room? How did it feel?
  3. Do people need a community, or can they be happy alone?
  4. What is the difference between being lonely and being alone?
  5. Which group do you belong to that you never chose?
  6. Would you rather fit in completely or stand out slightly?
