ESL Conversation Questions About Travel

Why we travel, how we behave when we do, and what we refuse to admit about being tourists. Twenty-four questions with plenty of storytelling built in.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Why we travel

  1. What are you actually looking for when you travel?
  2. Do you travel to rest, to learn, or to take photos?
  3. Is travel a hobby, an escape or a status symbol?
  4. What is the cheapest trip that changed you?
  5. Would you rather travel alone, with a partner or with friends?
  6. How many countries have you been to, and does that number matter?

Tourists versus travellers

  1. What is the difference between a tourist and a traveller?
  2. Is any of us really a traveller any more?
  3. Is Overtourism a local problem or a traveller’s problem?
  4. What behaviour abroad should be fined?
  5. Should you learn the language if you stay a week?
  6. What place was ruined for you by the other visitors?

Bad trips

  1. What is the worst trip you have ever taken?
  2. Have you ever been seriously lost in another country?
  3. What food did you regret trusting?
  4. Do you remember disasters more fondly than perfect trips?
  5. What would make you cancel a trip two days before?
  6. Which travel problem is worse: no money, no time or no company?

Living abroad

  1. Would you rather travel for a year or live abroad for six months?
  2. What do people underestimate about moving to another country?
  3. Which country would suit you, and which would defeat you?
  4. How long does it take to feel at home somewhere?
  5. When you move abroad, what do you miss that you did not expect?
  6. Would you move abroad for love?
