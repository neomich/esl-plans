ESL Conversation Questions About Technology

Phones, AI, privacy and the strange things we now do without thinking. These questions work for B1 and above, and they never run dry.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Phones and attention

  1. How many times a day do you check your phone without deciding to?
  2. What was the last thing you did that used to require a phone but no longer does?
  3. Do you feel anxious when your battery is below twenty percent?
  4. Should children be allowed smartphones before secondary school?
  5. What do you miss about life before smartphones?
  6. If you had to delete every app except three, which three survive?

Artificial intelligence

  1. Have you used AI for something you would be embarrassed to admit?
  2. Would you trust an AI doctor more than a tired human one?
  3. What job do you think AI will never do well?
  4. If your boss replaced you with AI, would you be angry or impressed?
  5. Should AI-generated content be labelled? Would you notice anyway?
  6. What would you ask an AI if it could answer only one question honestly?

Privacy

  1. What do you accept without reading, and why?
  2. Is privacy already dead, or do we just not care enough?
  3. Would you trade your data for a genuinely better service?
  4. What would you refuse to share, even for money?
  5. Do you cover your laptop camera? Be honest.
  6. Who has more information about you, your government or a tech company?

Digital life versus real life

  1. Is an online friendship a real friendship?
  2. What did the internet make better about your life?
  3. What did it quietly make worse?
  4. Would you rather lose your phone for a week or your bank card?
  5. Are people more honest online or offline?
  6. What will be normal in ten years that sounds absurd today?
