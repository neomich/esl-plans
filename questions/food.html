ESL Conversation Questions About Food

Comfort food, eating habits, restaurants and the things people are quietly judgemental about. Reliable for almost any level above A2.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Comfort and habits

  1. What food do you eat when you are not OK, and what does it fix?
  2. What do you eat every week because you were told it is healthy?
  3. Which food from your childhood tastes different now?
  4. Do you eat differently at home and alone than you would in front of others?
  5. What is the most pointless diet you have tried?
  6. If you had to eat one dish for a month, what would you choose?

Food culture

  1. Which country has the most overrated food?
  2. What is a normal food in your country that foreigners find shocking?
  3. Does food belong to a nation, or is that just branding?
  4. What is the strangest thing you have seen served with tea?
  5. Should a national dish mean the same thing to everyone who makes it?
  6. Which food tradition should disappear, and which should spread?

Restaurants

  1. What is the right way to complain in a restaurant?
  2. Is tipping fair to the customer, the waiter or neither?
  3. Do you trust a restaurant with no reviews?
  4. What makes a good waiter?
  5. Would you rather eat somewhere brilliant and crowded or empty and average?
  6. Have you ever left a restaurant because of the menu?

Cooking and money

  1. Who in your household actually cooks, and why?
  2. Is cooking a skill or a hobby?
  3. Has food become too expensive, or have we become too picky?
  4. What is worth paying extra for, and what is a waste?
  5. Would you rather never cook again or never eat out again?
  6. What dish do you make that would annoy a chef?
