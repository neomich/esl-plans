ESL Conversation Questions About Work

Careers, bosses, remote work and quitting — the questions adults actually answer honestly. Good for Business English lessons that should not feel like a meeting.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Career choices

  1. How did you end up doing what you do?
  2. What did you want to be at twelve, and what happened to that plan?
  3. Would you rather do something boring but secure or something exciting but unstable?
  4. Is it realistic to love your job?
  5. What job would you do for free for a year?
  6. Do you work to live or live to work?

Bosses and colleagues

  1. What makes a manager worth respecting?
  2. Have you ever had a boss you genuinely liked? What did they do differently?
  3. Is it possible to be friends with your boss?
  4. What office behaviour should be illegal but is not?
  5. How do you deal with a colleague who talks too much?
  6. Would you rather work with someone brilliant and unpleasant, or average and kind?

Remote work

  1. Are you more productive at home or in an office?
  2. What do you actually miss about the office?
  3. Should employers be allowed to track remote workers?
  4. Is working from home better for families, or just better for some families?
  5. What is the ideal number of days in the office?
  6. Would you take a pay cut to work remotely forever?

Quitting and ambition

  1. What would make you quit a job with no backup plan?
  2. Is job-hopping smart or suspicious?
  3. What does ambition look like when nobody is watching?
  4. Have you ever been relieved to be rejected?
  5. What is the most useful thing you learned from a job you hated?
  6. If money were solved, what would you do with your working hours?
