ESL Conversation Questions About Media

Fame, news, algorithms and what we actually watch. These questions work for everyone, and everyone answers them differently.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Fame

  1. Would you accept being famous if you had no money from it?
  2. What do people want when they want to be famous?
  3. Which celebrity would you be curious to have dinner with?
  4. Is it fair to criticise someone’s life choices when they chose fame?
  5. Who is the most famous person in your country, and what is that based on?
  6. Would you rather be respected by a hundred people or known by a million?

News

  1. How do you find out something important actually happened?
  2. Do you trust any single news source completely?
  3. Is the news a service or a business?
  4. What story do you think is underreported?
  5. What is the difference between an opinion and a lie?
  6. Would you pay for news if nothing else worked?

Feeds and algorithms

  1. What does your feed think you are interested in?
  2. Have you ever changed something about your life because a feed showed it to you?
  3. Is it wrong for a platform to decide what you feel like today?
  4. What content do you consume that you would be embarrassed to explain?
  5. Are we more divided because of social media, or just louder?
  6. Should there be a limit on how much of your attention can be sold?

Film, music and taste

  1. What film or album will always be part of who you are?
  2. Is taste in music about a person or about their age?
  3. Does anything deserve a remake?
  4. Why do adults care what teenagers listen to?
  5. What is too popular to be good, in your opinion?
  6. Would you rather discover something new or revisit something you love?
