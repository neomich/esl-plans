ESL Conversation Questions About Relationships

Friendship, family, dating and long-term love — 24 questions that get past "do you have a big family?" and into something people actually want to talk about.

24 questions · free to use, no signup · B1-C1

Friendships

  1. How many real friends do you have, honestly?
  2. What is the difference between a friend and a good acquaintance?
  3. Can men and women be close friends without complications?
  4. What would make you end a friendship you have had for years?
  5. Do friendships need effort, or do the real ones survive neglect?
  6. Who is the person you would call at three in the morning?

Family

  1. What is the most useful thing your parents taught you by accident?
  2. Do you become more like your parents as you get older?
  3. Is it possible to be too close to your family?
  4. Who in your family do you understand better now than five years ago?
  5. Should adult children live with their parents longer?
  6. What family tradition would you keep, and what would you quietly drop?

Dating

  1. What is the most important quality in a partner?
  2. Have dating apps made people more or less honest?
  3. What is a deal-breaker that others find unreasonable?
  4. Is it better to be loved or understood?
  5. Do people date differently at twenty and at forty?
  6. What is the worst first-date question you have been asked?

Long-term relationships

  1. What kills relationships faster than cheating?
  2. Is routine the enemy of romance?
  3. Should couples share money completely?
  4. What do people misunderstand about arranged marriages?
  5. Can a relationship survive living in different countries?
  6. What is the one conversation couples avoid for too long?
