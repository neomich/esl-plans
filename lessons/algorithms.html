What's the "Algorithms" ESL Lesson About?

B1-B2 · General English

Main Objectives

  • earn and apply basic culinary vocabulary to write clear, step-by-step instructions
  • understand the concept of an algorithm as a precise set of rules by analyzing a literal recipe interpretation and a targeted video explanation
  • evaluate the real-world impact of algorithms on daily life through a structured debate and a targeted podcast listening task

About This Lesson

Page 1: Culinary Vocabulary & Recipe Writing The lesson opens with a practical vocabulary task. Students match 12 common kitchen items (e.g., mayo, cucumber, loaf of bread, avocado, head of lettuce, tomatoes, kitchen knife, glass bowl, tin of tuna, tablespoon) to their corresponding pictures. They then use this vocabulary to write a simple, 3-sentence recipe, establishing the foundational idea of giving clear, sequential instructions. Page 2: The PB&J Algorithm & Video Explanation Students examine a flawed, overly literal interpretation of a recipe to understand how algorithms require exact, unambiguous steps to function correctly. They rewrite the confusing instructions to make them clear. Next, they watch a video where an expert explains the concept of an algorithm to a child using a peanut butter and jelly sandwich example. Students complete a True/False comprehension check based on the video, solidifying the definition of an algorithm as clear, step-by-step instructions used to solve a problem or complete a task. Page 3: Algorithms in Daily Life & Podcast Discussion The focus shifts to the broader impact of technology. Students review a 5-step e-commerce algorithm model (Data Collection, Profile Matching, Ranking and Sorting, Dynamic Triggers, Feedback Loop) and identify real-world examples, such as GPS routing, dating app matching, and dynamic airline pricing. They then evaluate 10 arguments for and against daily algorithm use (e.g., streamlining tedious tasks vs. exploiting psychology for screen time). Students listen to a podcast to verify their choices and hear the speakers' reasoning, concluding the lesson with a discussion on how these invisible rules shape personal choices and global systems. 📄 Inside This Lesson Pack 💬 Core Discussion Prompts & Speaking Activities Literal Instructions: Why did John’s recipe fail? How does this show the need for extreme precision when giving instructions to a computer or a machine? Algorithm Impact: Which of the 10 arguments for or against algorithms do you agree with the most? Do algorithms make your life easier or more controlled? Real-World Examples: Can you identify an algorithm you interact with daily (e.g., social media feeds, GPS, dating apps)? How does it change your behavior? 🧠 Target Lexicon & Vocabulary Focus Culinary Items: Mayo, bone-in steak, kitchen knife, cucumber, loaf of bread, avocado, head of lettuce, tomatoes, chopsticks, glass bowl, tin of tuna, tablespoon. Algorithm Steps: Data Collection, Profile Matching, Ranking and Sorting, Dynamic Triggers, Feedback Loop. Debate Language: Streamline tedious tasks, expand access to knowledge, flatten cultural diversity, exploit psychology, dictate information. 📺 Multimedia & Authentic Materials Included Page 2 Video Task: A targeted viewing exercise featuring an expert explaining the concept of an algorithm to a child using a peanut butter and jelly sandwich, paired with a True/False comprehension check. Page 3 Audio Task: A podcast discussion where speakers evaluate the positive and negative arguments of daily algorithm use, allowing students to verify their prior choices and hear native-level reasoning. ⚠️ Critical Thinking Extension Work Argument Evaluation: Students actively sort 10 statements about algorithm use into "positive" or "negative" categories before listening, then adjust their views based on the podcast evidence. Real-World Mapping: Students select one hidden background algorithm (e.g., high-frequency stock market trading, city traffic light timing, or credit scoring) and explain its step-by-step process using the 5-step e-commerce model as a template.
