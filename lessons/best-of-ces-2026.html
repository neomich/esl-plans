Best of CES 2026

🎓 General English 🥉 B1 ⏱ 90 min ▶️🎵 Video & Audio
Technology

Main Objectives

  • Discuss personal preferences and opinions regarding modern gadgets and consumer electronics;
  • Learn and practice advanced technology-related verbs (e.g., automate, deploy, generate, trigger) through definition matching and contextual gap-fills;
  • Evaluate the features and potential impact of innovative CES 2026 products through reading comprehension and true/false verification.

About This Lesson

In this lesson, students begin by discussing their excitement about upcoming tech, identifying which gadgets could change their daily routines or make good gifts, and debating which inventions are truly useful versus completely unnecessary. They then expand their lexical range by matching 12 powerful action verbs (such as automate, predict, deploy, and generate) to their definitions, establishing a framework for describing what modern technology can do. Next, learners explore a showcase of the "Best of CES 2026," reading detailed descriptions of cutting-edge products like the Samsung Galaxy Z TriFold, the Roborock Saros Rover, and the LG CLOiD robot. They apply new vocabulary through gap-fill exercises. Finally, students verify true/false statements about specific innovations — such as Lenovo's rollable laptops, Boston Dynamics' Atlas robot, and Samsung's new OLED TVs — before using the target verbs to describe the gadgets they use most frequently.

What's Included

PDF handout, audio materials (MP3), video links, full downloadable package (RAR). Everything you need to run this lesson is ready to download — zero preparation required.

Who Is This For?

This lesson plan is designed for online ESL tutors working with adult learners at B1 level. It uses a conversation-driven approach with authentic materials to keep adult students genuinely engaged throughout the session.
