C1 ESL Lesson Plans for Adults

35 C1 ESL lesson plans for adults — advanced conversation and discussion topics with PDF, audio and video. Zero prep for online tutors.

What a C1 lesson plan has to do

C1 students are the easiest to bore and the hardest to impress. They have opinions, they have vocabulary, and they can smell a textbook exercise from across the room. A C1 lesson only works if the topic is genuinely difficult — an ethical trade-off, an uncomfortable statistic, a position they have never had to defend.

C1 discussion topics that survive advanced students

The topics here are the ones adults argue about when the small talk is over: status, debt, authenticity, attention, the ethics of work, what progress actually costs. Nothing is "advanced" because the words are long; it is advanced because there is no safe answer. If you only want the questions, start with the free conversation-question sets.

C1 speaking lesson plans and exam-style tasks

Plans include a PDF, audio and video, and are built for 90-minute one-to-one sessions, though most split cleanly into two. You get discussion, a listening or reading text with real difficulty, and language work that targets precision — collocation, connotation, register — rather than another round of conditionals.

Why there is no "advanced grammar" lesson here

At C1, grammar is rarely the bottleneck. Precision is. A student who says "the government should invest more" is grammatically fine and communicatively vague. The lessons work on the second problem, because that is the one that shows up in a meeting, an interview or an exam.
