A2 ESL lesson plans for adults

A2 students can survive a shop, a station and a short story about their weekend. What they cannot do yet is keep a conversation going once the easy questions run out. Every A2 plan here is built around one situation and one set of phrases they will actually need — not a vocabulary list to memorise and forget.

A2 conversation topics that do not feel childish

Adults at A2 are not children at A2. The topics are food, travel, money, work, habits and small everyday decisions, because that is what a forty-year-old talks about. The language is simple; the subject is not.

How these A2 plans work online

Each plan includes a PDF handout, audio and video, and is designed for a 60-minute one-to-one lesson over a screen share. No printing. The questions are short enough to answer with the grammar the student already has, and there is always an extension task for the student who turns out to be stronger than the level label.
