B2 ESL Lesson Plans for Adults

91 B2 ESL lesson plans for adults: conversation, speaking and discussion topics with PDF, audio and video. Zero prep for online tutors.

What a B2 ESL lesson plan has to do

B2 is the level where students stop needing the language and start needing a reason to use it. They can already explain their job, tell a story and disagree politely. What they cannot always do is hold a position under pressure, notice register, or say the same thing three different ways. So every B2 plan here is built around one argument or one decision, not a vocabulary list.

B2 ESL conversation questions

Conversation is the engine. The questions move from concrete to uncomfortable: first what happened, then what you think, then what you would defend in front of someone who disagrees. If you only want the questions, the free conversation-question sets cover psychology, work, travel, food, technology, society, relationships and media.

B2 speaking lesson plans for online tutors

These are made for one-to-one online teaching: a screen-shareable PDF, links that open in a browser, nothing to print. A typical B2 lesson runs 60-90 minutes — warm-up, core task, feedback you can actually get through. Teaching in 45-minute blocks? Split the plan at the second page; each half still works on its own.

What B2 students usually still get wrong

Not grammar. Register is the big one — "kids" in a job interview, "furthermore" in a bar. Then hedging: B2 students state opinions like facts. Both are easier to fix inside a topic they care about than inside a grammar exercise, which is the whole point of this library.
