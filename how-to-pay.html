💳 How to Pay

Tribute is a secure payment service that lets you subscribe to ESL-plans.com. Here are simple steps to make your payment with total peace of mind!

1

Choose Bank Card as your payment method (if you are outside Russia). All major cards are accepted — Visa, Mastercard and more.

2

You will be prompted to sign in to complete your payment. If you have a Telegram account, you can use it. If not — no worries! Simply select E-mail to receive a verification code and continue with your payment.

3

Enter your email address. Any valid email will work — it doesn't need to match your ESL-plans account.

4

Check your inbox for the verification code. Once received, enter the 4-digit code and you will be redirected back to complete your payment.

5

Select the country of your bank card. This helps ensure your payment is processed correctly. Choose "Select another country" if your country isn't listed.

6

Enter your card details and click Pay. Your subscription status on ESL-plans.com will update shortly after payment. Thank you for your purchase — I hope you and your students have a wonderful time using the materials! 🎉
